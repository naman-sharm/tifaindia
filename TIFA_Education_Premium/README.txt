TIFA Education Premium Website (Direct HTML)

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract this ZIP completely.
2. Keep index.html, css/ and js/ together in the same folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or another browser.

FEATURES
- Responsive desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile layout
- Sticky navigation and mobile menu
- Scroll reveal animations and animated number counters
- Hover interactions, course category filters and course enquiry preselection
- WhatsApp enquiry form and floating WhatsApp shortcut
- TIFA's published course list, contact details and six listed centres
- Official TIFA gallery photographs embedded from tifaindia.com

BRAND / LOGO NOTE
The official TIFA logo is stored locally as images/tifaindia-logo.png and used in the header, footer and browser tab. Keeping the logo local avoids depending on the official website for page rendering.

IMPORTANT
- Internet is required for Google Fonts, official TIFA gallery images and WhatsApp links.
- The course figures and placement statistics are labelled as claims from TIFA's website, not independently verified.
- Branch details are transcribed from TIFA's published contact page. Confirm addresses and opening hours with TIFA before publishing.
- The enquiry form opens WhatsApp; it does not store submissions in a database.
- To deploy online, upload the whole folder to your hosting provider (e.g. Vercel static site or Netlify). Do not upload only index.html.
