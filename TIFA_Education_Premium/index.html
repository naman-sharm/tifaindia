TIFA Education Institute has been focused on skill development and career-oriented training since 2010. Formerly known as Balaji Computer, the institute aims to help learners build practical knowledge and prepare for professional opportunities.

From accounting fundamentals and taxation to business software and digital marketing, TIFA's course portfolio is built around useful skills and workplace-oriented learning.
